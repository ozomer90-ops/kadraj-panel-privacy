Kadraj Publishing Panel

A private, single-user desktop tool that the channel owner runs on their own computer to publish videos to their own three YouTube channels: Kadraj Dışı, FramePerfect Oz and ŞipŞap TV.

  • Uploads the owner's own videos and schedules publish times, using YouTube API Services.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and playlists. Every upload needs manual approval.
  • Reads the owner's own channel analytics.
  • Not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else. No sign-up, no other users.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
